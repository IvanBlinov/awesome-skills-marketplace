## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- `researcher` agent: splits a question into sub-questions, reads primary sources and
  writes a cited report with confidence levels.
- `source-citation` skill: source quality, citation format, confidence levels and
  untrusted-content rules, reusable by other plugins.
- `research_dir` option (default `docs/research`).
